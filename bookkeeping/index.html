Questions

Quick answers

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many bank and card accounts you have, how many transactions a month, and extras like payroll or bill pay. Most small businesses land between $300 and $1,000 a month. You'll get a fixed number after the check-up, before you commit to anything. See the packages.

I'm months (or years) behind. Is that a problem?

Not at all, it's very common. We quote a one-time catch-up price first, then you move onto the monthly service with clean books.

Do you do taxes?

No. We keep the books and hand your tax preparer a clean year-end package. If you don't have a CPA, we can point you to one.

Do I have to use QuickBooks?

We work in QuickBooks Online. If you're on something else (or a spreadsheet), we can help you move over as part of setup.
