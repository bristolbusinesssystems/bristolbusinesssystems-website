Brand Guidelines

Bristol Business Systems

Built for the gateway to Vermont’s Green Mountains. Trusted systems, honest service, and a visual palette rooted in forest, stone, and calm sky.

Primary Brand Mark

The icon is the core mark. Use it with ample clearspace and pair with the wordmark for official identity applications.

Logo Variations

Maintain contrast and legibility across applications. Use dark, light, and monochrome versions depending on background support.

Dark background version
White-on-dark or full-color mark for on-brand panels.
Light background version
Neutral on light surfaces with strong readability.
Accent background version
High-impact use on gradients, cards, and hero blocks.

Brand Palette

A grounded palette inspired by Vermont’s mountains: deep evergreens, soft stone, pale sky, and a warm accent for clarity and focus.

Logo Clearspace

Always surround the mark with clear, unobstructed space. No text, imagery, or visual elements should infringe on this boundary.

Minimum clearspace equals the height of the mark’s container. Use this margin in all layouts, digital headers, print, and signage.

For compact applications, prioritize the icon version only when the full wordmark cannot fit legibly.

Typography

Modern and professional with a clear hierarchy. Use a strong display font for headings and a neutral sans serif for supporting text.

Buttons should feel solid and inviting. Use gradient primary buttons for calls to action and subtle outlines for secondary actions.
