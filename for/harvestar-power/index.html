What the records show

The market got smaller. Your share of it got smaller faster.

I pulled every net-metering case the Public Utility Commission has for Lincoln, Bristol, Starksboro, Monkton and New Haven. That's 1,221 filings covering 856 solar sites, each with its installer, size, date and whether it's owned or leased.

0 of 28

2026 filings in your five towns list Harvestar. In 2024 you had 14 of 68, second only to SunCommon. Your last one locally was November 2025.

35 → 10

Your filings statewide, January to October, 2024 vs 2026. This year's work moved to Charlotte, Middlebury and the Mad River Valley.

173

Different customers have listed Harvestar as installer since 2017. That's a referral base most installers would love to have.

142

Local systems with no installer looking after them. 103 were put in by companies that left Vermont or went bankrupt, and 39 more by installers that look inactive.

The 28 jobs filed locally in 2026 went to Building Energy, Solar Zack, SunCommon and Team Sunshine. That's not a criticism. It shows the work near home is still there, and someone's getting it. Notes: filing dates are close to install dates but not the same. Sunrun no longer lists Vermont among its states but still owns its leased systems here.

Fair questions

What you might be wondering.

Where did the numbers about my towns come from?

Public Vermont PUC net-metering records. Anyone can search them one case at a time on the ePUC site. We wrote software to pull and sort all 1,221 cases for your five towns and checked which installers are still in business.

Is it OK to contact people from public records?

Yes, by mail and in your name. We'd keep it to a useful, honest offer, like a checkup for a system whose installer is gone, and keep the list private to you.

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. The fixes in the website section work on your current WordPress site. A rebuild is an option later, not a starting point.

Couldn't I just do this myself?

The website fixes, yes, if you have the time. The data pulls, the calculator, the outage automation and the mailing lists are build work. That's the part we do so you can stay on roofs and in basements.

What would it cost?
