Local plan

More people through the Williston door.

Two jobs: fill Saturdays and Sundays from 11 to 5, and get Vermonters offering you pieces to buy.

Free basics first

  • Google Business Profile with weekend hours, booking link, 20+ photos and a weekly “just arrived” post
  • A Google review request for every local pickup
  • A showroom page for searches like “vintage furniture Burlington VT”

Vermont channels

  • Front Porch Forum posts in Williston, Burlington, South Burlington, Shelburne, Essex and Charlotte
  • Seven Days ads and a story pitch built on your restorations
  • Selected pieces on Facebook Marketplace, pointing to the showroom

Partnerships

  • Real estate agents and stagers: staging loans and closing-gift vouchers
  • Interior designers: trade pricing and private previews
  • Ski condo and lake house owners in Stowe and Shelburne: furnishing packages

Showroom events and sourcing

  • A monthly First Look Saturday, announced to your list first
  • A short talk such as “How to spot real Danish teak”
  • A “We buy mid-century furniture” page and estate-sale contacts
