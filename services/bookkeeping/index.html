FAQ

Common questions

Why a flat fee instead of hourly?

Hourly billing puts you and your bookkeeper on opposite sides: the slower the work, the bigger the bill. A flat fee means we're rewarded for being efficient, and you get the same predictable bill every month.

How do you set the price?

Mainly by the number of bank and card accounts, how many transactions you have each month, and extras like payroll, invoicing, bill pay, inventory or sales tax. We look at your actual books before giving a final number.

What does a catch-up cost?

It depends on how far behind you are and what shape the books are in. We give you a fixed quote after reviewing the file, and ask for half up front and half on completion.

Do I need to be on QuickBooks Online?

Yes, we work in QuickBooks Online. You own the subscription (billed by Intuit), so the books stay yours no matter what. If you're on QuickBooks Desktop, another program or spreadsheets, moving you over can be part of setup.

Do you do taxes or payroll?

We don't prepare tax returns; we hand your CPA clean books and a year-end package. For payroll, your payroll provider runs the checks and we record everything correctly in your books each pay period.

Will you need to come to my office?

No. Everything works remotely through QuickBooks Online and your bank feeds. If you're local to Bristol and would like to meet in person, we're happy to.

Is there a long contract?

No long-term contract. The engagement letter explains how either side can end the service with notice, and your books stay in your own QuickBooks file.

How is my financial information protected?

Access is through your own QuickBooks user permissions, which you can remove at any time. Logins are kept in an encrypted password vault, and we don't upload your financial data to outside AI services.
